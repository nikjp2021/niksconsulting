# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Startup founders and SME owners exploring AI consulting services. They're busy, skeptical of buzzwords, and want to see proof before committing. They visit from LinkedIn referrals, Google searches, or direct outreach. Context: evaluating whether Niks Consulting is credible enough for a conversation.

International students seeking real-world project experience through the Gifted Internship Program. They're ambitious, globally minded, and looking for hands-on mentorship over classroom theory.

## Product Purpose

Niks Consulting is an AI-first consulting firm that helps startups and SMEs implement AI solutions, automate operations, and execute digital transformation. The website is the primary credibility signal: it must demonstrate expertise through its own design quality. If the site looks generic, the consulting proposition fails.

Success looks like: a visitor lands, understands what Niks Consulting does within 5 seconds, sees proof of results, and either books a consultation or applies to the Gifted program.

## Brand Personality

**Expert, warm, bold.**

Not corporate-sterile. Not startup-casual. The brand speaks with the confidence of someone who's shipped real AI systems (Xiaomi MiMo Grant winner, Expedia-scale projects) but explains things like a trusted advisor, not a pitch deck.

Three words: **confident, human, technical.**

Emotional goals: trust, excitement about possibilities, confidence in the team's ability to deliver.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (Inter font, blue gradient hero, identical card grids)
- Dark-mode-everything tech bro aesthetics
- Over-the-top agency sites with parallax overload and no substance
- Corporate consulting sites (McKinsey/Bain clone aesthetics)
- AI hype pages with neural network visuals and no proof

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** Every claim needs proof. Case studies with real numbers beat taglines.
2. **Expertise through craft.** The site's own design quality is evidence of the team's capability.
3. **Warm professionalism.** Technical credibility without coldness. The Gifted program and team bios add human warmth.
4. **Progressive depth.** Hero gives the headline, scroll reveals proof, pages go deep for those who want it.
5. **Global-local.** Bilingual-ready, culturally aware, serving 8 countries from Japan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance minimum
- Full dark/light theme support
- Reduced motion respected
- Touch targets 44x44px minimum
- Semantic HTML throughout
